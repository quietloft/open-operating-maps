# 2. Offer

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** founder / commercial lead

## Objective
Define exactly what is sold: scope, price, promise, guarantee, and what is
explicitly excluded. An offer that needs a call to be understood is not an offer.

## Inputs
- positioning sentence (area 1)
- delivery cost and time, measured — not estimated
- the last 10 proposals and their outcome

## Process
1. Name the outcome the customer gets, in their words.
2. List deliverables. Cut anything not needed for the outcome.
3. Fix the price from cost and margin, never from what "feels right".
4. Write what is excluded. This is the part that prevents disputes.
5. Add one risk-reversal (guarantee, pilot, exit condition).
6. Publish it on one page with a single call to action.

## Tools
- Primary: a quote template that computes margin
- Support: payment link, contract template

## Owner
Commercial lead proposes; founder approves price changes.

## KPIs
- Leading: quote-to-close rate, average time to quote
- Lagging: gross margin per deal
- Review: monthly

## Failure modes
- **Custom every time** → symptom: no deal resembles another → fix: one standard
  tier, one clearly priced custom path
- **Excluded scope unwritten** → symptom: scope creep, unhappy client → fix:
  exclusions in the quote, initialled
- **Price from competitor** → symptom: margin below 30% → fix: price from cost

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Every open quote has a defined expiry date
- [ ] Margin on each quote checked, not assumed
- [ ] Any lost deal: was it price, scope, or trust? Recorded?
