# 1. Positioning

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** founder / head of marketing

## Objective
State, in one sentence, who the customer is, the alternative they would use
without us, and the single reason we win. Written by the founder, not delegated.

## Inputs
- 5-10 real customer conversations (or 20 support tickets read end to end)
- a list of 3 named competitors with their price and promise
- what customers say *before* they buy (the trigger), not after

## Process
1. Write the customer in one line: role, context, the constraint that hurts.
2. Write the alternative with a brand name, including "do nothing" and
   "an intern with a spreadsheet".
3. List every reason we could win. Cross all but one.
4. Test the sentence: replace our name with a competitor's. If it still reads
   true, it is not positioning.
5. Put the sentence at the top of the website, the deck and the first email.

## Tools
- Primary: one shared document, versioned in git
- Support: interview notes, competitor screenshots

## Owner
The founder decides alone. Marketing executes and may challenge with evidence.

## KPIs
- Leading: 8 of 10 new leads can repeat our sentence back
- Lagging: win rate vs the named alternative
- Review: quarterly, or when win rate drops 10 points

## Failure modes
- **"Everyone can use it"** → symptom: no one repeats the message → fix: name the
  customer in the sentence, accept losing the others
- **Positioning written by committee** → symptom: a sentence with three "and" →
  fix: founder writes, one sentence, one reason
- **Positioning never reaches the site** → symptom: homepage says something else
  entirely → fix: the homepage headline *is* the sentence

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Can I say the sentence from memory?
- [ ] Does the homepage headline match it word for word?
- [ ] Did any sales call lose on a reason we thought we won on? Write it down.
