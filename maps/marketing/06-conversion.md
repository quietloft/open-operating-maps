# 6. Conversion

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** growth lead

## Objective
Remove the reasons a motivated visitor leaves without acting. Measured as a rate,
not an opinion.

## Inputs
- a funnel with numbers at each step (arrive → engage → act)
- 5 session recordings or 10 user questions
- page load time on a mobile connection

## Process
1. Write the funnel with real numbers. Find the step with the biggest drop.
2. List the three most likely reasons for that drop. Rank by evidence.
3. Change one thing; keep the change for at least one full week or 200 visits.
4. Record the result in the same table as before.
5. Keep the change only if the rate moved; otherwise revert.

## Tools
- Primary: analytics with event tracking
- Support: session recordings, a results table

## Owner
Growth lead decides; nobody redesigns a converting page without data.

## KPIs
- Leading: conversion rate per step
- Lagging: cost per acquired customer
- Review: weekly

## Failure modes
- **Redesign by taste** → symptom: pretty site, lower rate → fix: one change at a
  time, revert on no movement
- **No baseline** → symptom: cannot tell if it improved → fix: record the current
  rate before touching anything
- **Form asking too much** → symptom: high engagement, low submit → fix: one
  field, one action

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Funnel numbers updated for the week
- [ ] The test running has a defined end date
- [ ] Mobile load time still acceptable
