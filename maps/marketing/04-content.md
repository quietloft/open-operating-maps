# 4. Content

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** content lead

## Objective
Publish on a schedule that survives a bad week, in one format that can be
produced repeatedly, for the one customer defined in area 1.

## Inputs
- content calendar with the next 4 weeks filled
- a bank of at least 20 raw ideas (not drafts)
- the production time per asset, measured

## Process
1. Choose one primary channel and one format. Everything else is optional.
2. Set cadence from measured production time, not ambition: if an asset takes
   90 minutes, three per week is 4.5 hours — decide if that exists.
3. Batch: write in one session, produce in another, schedule in a third.
4. Every asset ends with the same call to action.
5. Monthly: kill the format with the lowest saves/replies, keep the best.

## Tools
- Primary: scheduler for the channel
- Support: idea bank, asset archive with naming convention

## Owner
Content lead owns cadence; nobody publishes outside the calendar without asking.

## KPIs
- Leading: assets published vs planned (target 90%)
- Lagging: saves and replies per asset
- Review: monthly

## Failure modes
- **Four channels, none alive** → symptom: last post on one is 3 weeks old →
  fix: one channel, cadence that survives a bad week
- **Ideas die as drafts** → symptom: 30 drafts, 3 published → fix: bank of raw
  ideas, batch production, publish anyway
- **Content unrelated to the offer** → symptom: reach up, sales flat → fix: every
  asset ends with the offer's call to action

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Next week is scheduled and ready
- [ ] Published vs planned recorded
- [ ] Best and worst asset of the week: why?
