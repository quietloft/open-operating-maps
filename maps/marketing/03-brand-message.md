# 3. Brand & Message

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** creative lead

## Objective
Make the same promise, in the same voice, everywhere a customer meets us — and
make it recognisable without the logo.

## Inputs
- positioning sentence and offer (areas 1-2)
- the 5 places a customer actually sees us (audit, not memory)

## Process
1. Write three proof points that support the promise. Each must be checkable.
2. Fix the voice rules: three do's, three don'ts, one example of each.
3. Define the visual minimum: one typeface, two colours, one wordmark placement.
4. Apply to the five touchpoints found in the audit. Stop there.
5. Re-audit quarterly against the same five.

## Tools
- Primary: one brand file (text, not a 60-page PDF)
- Support: template for social covers, signature, proposal header

## Owner
Creative lead decides; founder approves the promise only.

## KPIs
- Leading: consistency score across the five touchpoints (checklist, 0-10)
- Lagging: unaided recall in customer conversations

## Failure modes
- **Brand book nobody opens** → symptom: each post looks like a different company
  → fix: one page, five touchpoints, checked quarterly
- **Voice described in adjectives** ("bold, premium") → symptom: nobody can apply
  it → fix: show one paragraph written correctly, one written wrongly

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Last published asset matches the voice rules
- [ ] Wordmark placement identical to the previous asset
- [ ] Any new claim backed by a proof point
