# 5. Acquisition

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** growth lead

## Objective
Bring strangers in through one or two channels with a measured cost, and know
which one to double.

## Inputs
- offer and conversion path (areas 2, 6) — do not buy traffic into a leak
- baseline: current visits, sources, cost per lead
- tracking that survives a privacy-restricted browser

## Process
1. Start with the channel where the customer already searches or scrolls.
2. Run one campaign with one variable changed at a time.
3. Measure cost per *qualified* lead, not per click.
4. Kill anything with no result in 30 days; double the one with the best cost.
5. Keep a written log: date, channel, spend, result.

## Tools
- Primary: one ads or search console account
- Support: UTM naming convention, spend log

## Owner
Growth lead owns spend; increases above the agreed budget need approval.

## KPIs
- Leading: cost per qualified lead, by channel
- Lagging: customer acquisition cost vs first-year margin
- Review: weekly while a campaign is live

## Failure modes
- **Traffic into a broken page** → symptom: clicks high, leads zero → fix: fix
  conversion (area 6) before increasing spend
- **No written log** → symptom: "we tried that, it didn't work" with no numbers
  → fix: spend log from day one
- **Channel hopping weekly** → symptom: no channel has 30 days of data → fix: one
  channel, one month, then judge

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Spend and leads per channel recorded
- [ ] Cost per qualified lead computed
- [ ] Anything running without a result for 30 days? Stop it.
