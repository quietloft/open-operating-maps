# 8. Measurement

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** growth lead

## Objective
Know which numbers are real, which are assumptions, and what decision each number
changes. A dashboard nobody acts on is decoration.

## Inputs
- the 3-5 numbers that actually drive decisions
- one source of truth per number
- a place where they are written weekly

## Process
1. Choose at most five numbers. For each, write what decision it changes.
2. Fix one source per number; if two tools disagree, write down which one wins.
3. Record them weekly, same day, same order.
4. Every month, delete a metric nobody used to decide anything.
5. Keep a separate list of assumptions you have not measured.

## Tools
- Primary: one sheet or database, written by hand is fine
- Support: analytics, ad accounts, payment provider

## Owner
Growth lead consolidates; each area owner supplies their number.

## KPIs
- Leading: metrics recorded on time (target 100% of weeks)
- Lagging: decisions changed by data (count them)

## Failure modes
- **Twenty-metric dashboard** → symptom: nobody opens it → fix: five numbers
- **Two tools, two truths** → symptom: "the analytics say otherwise" → fix: declare
  the winning source, write it down
- **Numbers without decisions** → symptom: reporting for its own sake → fix: every
  metric has a written decision attached

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Five numbers recorded, same day
- [ ] Any number that moved: why, in one line
- [ ] Assumption list updated with anything newly measured
