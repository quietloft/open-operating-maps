# 7. Retention

**Department:** Marketing · **Owner:** customer lead

## Objective
Increase the value of a customer already acquired: repeat purchase, referral,
or honest feedback that prevents churn.

## Inputs
- list of customers with purchase dates
- the moment of highest satisfaction (usually just after delivery)
- churn or non-renewal reasons, where known

## Process
1. Define the one moment to ask for something (review, referral, upsell).
2. Write one message per moment. One action per message.
3. Send on a schedule, not when remembered.
4. Log every response and every repeat purchase.
5. Quarterly: call five customers who did not come back. Ask why.

## Tools
- Primary: email or messaging list
- Support: simple customer table with dates

## Owner
Customer lead owns the schedule; founder makes the calls that need a human.

## KPIs
- Leading: repeat purchase rate within 90 days
- Lagging: revenue per customer, referral share of new business
- Review: monthly

## Failure modes
- **All effort on new customers** → symptom: acquisition cost rises every quarter
  → fix: one scheduled touch after delivery
- **Asking everything at once** (review + referral + upsell) → symptom: nothing
  happens → fix: one ask per message
- **Never calling churned customers** → symptom: reasons invented internally →
  fix: five calls per quarter

## Checklist (10 minutes, weekly)
- [ ] Post-delivery messages sent on schedule
- [ ] Repeat purchases logged
- [ ] One churned customer contacted this month
